A Letter For You...

Please turn up your volume, and take your time.

I know you.

You're the eldest of two children.
One part of you learned to carry the weight of the world,
while another just wants a safe place to rest.

I know you.

You say you don't deserve love,
but I see a heart that is so incredibly worthy of being held safely.

I know you.

You keep looking for someone who will look at everything you've done, everything you've achieved, and simply say,

"I'm proud of you."

I know you.

You're strong. You're independent.
You spend your days saving lives.

But when it feels like the world is falling apart, or like you're completely on your own, remember—
I'll be the person willing to cross oceans just to reach you.

I know you.

I know you're carrying pain you rarely talk about.
But being strong doesn't mean you have to carry everything alone.

And whatever made you believe you deserve that pain—
you don't.

I'm not moving on. I'll give you space and keep my distance for a while, but I'll be here, quietly hoping you find your way back.

I made you a promise to love you always and forever, and that's not something I say lightly.

I still believe our story isn't finished.
I believe in us.
